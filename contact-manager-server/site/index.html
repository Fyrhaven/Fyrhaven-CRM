Contact Manager Cloud

Your contact records, on a server that is not your browser

The Contact Manager plugin keeps projects, private notes, consent records and its change history as records. They can live on your own mail server — or here, so they follow you between devices and can be shared with the people you work with.

This is the same service your plugin talks to. Your workspace token is a password to it: never paste it anywhere else.
